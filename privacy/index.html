Privacy policy

Effective November 24, 2026

Jitz is made by MoZa Enterprises LLC (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the Jitz app.

The short version

Your training data stays on your phone. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, crash reporting or tracking. We don’t sell or share your data.

What Jitz stores, and where

Jitz stores your training data in a local database on your device: your logs, notes, techniques, training partners’ names as you type them, belt history and settings.

We don’t run servers that receive this data, so we can’t see it.

What we don’t collect

Jitz has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking. We don’t sell or share any data.

Voice dictation

Voice dictation uses your phone’s built-in speech recognition: Apple’s on iPhone and Google’s on Android. Depending on your device, the audio may be processed by Apple’s or Google’s speech service, under their privacy policies. Jitz doesn’t store recordings.

Backups and exports

When you export a backup or a spreadsheet, the file goes only where you choose to save or share it.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app deletes its data on your device. If you want to keep your training history, export a backup first.

Children

Jitz is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We plan to add optional features later, such as AI summaries of your notes (your notes would be sent to our server and to Anthropic’s API to create them) and optional accounts with sync. These aren’t in the app today. Before any feature like this ships, we’ll update this policy and its effective date.
