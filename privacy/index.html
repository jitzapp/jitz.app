Privacy policy

Effective November 27, 2026

Jitz is made by MoZa Enterprises LLC (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the Jitz app.

The short version

Your training data stays on your phone. Jitz shares anonymous usage counts to help us improve the app, and you can turn this off in Settings. There are no accounts, ads or tracking. We don’t sell your data.

What Jitz stores, and where

Jitz stores your training data in a local database on your device: your logs, notes, techniques, training partners’ names as you type them, belt history and settings.

We don’t run servers that receive this data, so we can’t see it.

Anonymous usage

Unless you turn off Share anonymous usage in Settings, Jitz sends anonymous counts to PostHog, our analytics provider, to help us understand how the app is used. These include when the app is opened, when logs are saved and which features are used (for example voice, attachments or search), along with your app version, device platform and a random ID created by the app. They never include your notes, names, techniques, partners, searches, links or anything you type. They aren’t linked to you, we don’t keep your IP address, and we don’t use them for advertising. Turning the setting off stops sending immediately.

What we don’t collect

Jitz has no accounts, no ads and no tracking across other apps or websites. We don’t sell your data or share it for advertising.

Voice dictation

Voice dictation uses your phone’s built-in speech recognition: Apple’s on iPhone and Google’s on Android. Depending on your device, the audio may be processed by Apple’s or Google’s speech service, under their privacy policies. Jitz doesn’t store recordings.

Photos, videos and links

Photos and videos you attach to a log stay in your phone’s photo library. Jitz saves a reference to them, not a copy, so they’re only visible on your phone. Jitz only accesses the items you pick. Links you attach are saved with your log on your phone.

Backups and exports

When you export a backup or a spreadsheet, the file goes only where you choose to save or share it.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app deletes its data on your device. If you want to keep your training history, export a backup first. To stop anonymous usage counts, turn off Share anonymous usage in Settings.

Children

Jitz is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We plan to add optional features later, such as AI summaries of your notes (your notes would be sent to our server and to Anthropic’s API to create them) and optional accounts with sync. These aren’t in the app today. Before any feature like this ships, we’ll update this policy and its effective date.
