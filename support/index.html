Common questions

How do I back up my data or move it to a new phone?

In Jitz, go to Settings › Your data › Export backup and save the file somewhere your new phone can reach, like a cloud drive or an email to yourself. On the new phone, install Jitz and go to Settings › Your data › Restore from backup. This works between iPhone and Android too.

Do I need an account?

No. Open the app and start logging.

Does Jitz work offline?

Yes. Your data lives on your phone, so you can log and look back at your training without a connection.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app, which deletes its data on your device. Restoring a backup also replaces what’s there. If you want to keep your history, export a backup first.

Can I get my training into a spreadsheet?

Yes. Jitz can export your training as a spreadsheet file.

When can I get Jitz?

Jitz is in beta now. On iPhone, join through TestFlight. On Android, join through Google Play.
